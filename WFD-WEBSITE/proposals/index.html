/ The Insight

Most cannabis designers
solve "cannabis."
We solve the category
you're competing in.

The low-dose social occasion market isn't competing against Kiva or Wana. It's competing against craft cocktails, luxury wellness products, and the curated objects people choose to put on their countertops.

That positioning changes everything — the typography has to feel editorial, the color palette has to feel considered, the hierarchy has to communicate sophistication before it communicates dosage.

The mint form factor challenge: a tin or slider gives you roughly 2 square inches of visual real estate to communicate "premium adult brand" without reading pharmaceutical and without reading dispensary. Every element is load-bearing — wordmark weight, color blocking, surface finish, and how compliance callouts get integrated without killing the premium signal. Get one element wrong and the whole thing reads cheap.
