## What would flip a no

- A drop-in around $8-10 or a 5-visit pack closer to $40, plus seeing on the free visit that the Baby Nook really keeps the little ones safe from the bigger kids.
- A drop-in around $8 a kid, or a cheap weekday-morning rate that works out to about $5 a visit on a pack.
- A real moms-group rate that makes it around $5 a kid, or a free Group Morning trial for my whole group so I could see it is actually better.
- Reviews from other parents confirming it's really clean, plus a cheaper drop-in around $10 or a pack that brings the per-visit cost down.
- A drop-in around $8 to $10 for a parent and one kid, or a free-entry weekday morning once in a while.
- A weekend session just for under-2s or a clear cap on numbers I can see on Saturdays, and a drop-in price closer to $10.
- A weekend price closer to $8-10 a kid, or a free first visit that is actually good and doesn't feel crowded on a Saturday.
- A free or very cheap parent-and-kid session, like a pay-what-you-can morning, or a long-term price under $8 a visit.
- A Groupon or intro deal like 3 visits for $20, or a 5-visit pack closer to $40 that doesn't expire quickly.
- A Groupon or a launch deal like a 5-visit pack for around $40, or drop-in at $10 on weekday mornings.
- A drop-in price around $8 per child, or a rainy-week deal I could use a couple of times without committing to a pack.
- A drop-in around $8 to $10 for the first kid, or a cheap pack where it works out to under $8 a visit, and it being close enough that gas isn't a factor.

Buyers say they would buy **1.8 times** in the first month on average.
