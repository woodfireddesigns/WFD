# Little Shore Play Cafe · One page

**Status: concept, pre-test. Verdict from the numbers: Not yet.**

**The problem.** Salisbury parents of under-5s run out of indoor places to go. The options in town are mixed-age bounce venues (CoCo's, Altitude) or short scheduled freebies (a 30-minute library storytime). No play cafe exists within 30 miles; the nearest are in Ocean City, Easton and Dover.

**The customer.** Parents home with an under-5 on weekdays, remote and hybrid workers, moms-group organizers and birthday planners in Salisbury and Wicomico County.

**The offer and price.** A calm, clean, Montessori-inspired play village for ages 8 months to 5, with a gated Baby Nook. Parents stay on site with coffee and Wi-Fi.

- First visit free
- Drop-in $14 with house coffee included; siblings $7; under 1 free
- 5-visit pack $55
- Membership $45 a month, month to month; founding families $39, locked for 12 months (first 50)
- Parties from $295, all-in

**The evidence.**

- Simulated buyer panel (100): 24% buy as first pitched, nearly all as drop-ins. The revised offer drew 45% on a 20-buyer re-test. Simulated, so treated as an upper bound.
- Competitor gap: no calm, under-5, parent-seating play cafe in the county. Local drop-ins run $18 to $23; Little Shore is $14 with coffee.
- Real-world test: not yet run. Pop-ups run Feb to May 2027.

**What the money is for.** The pop-up test now (~$2,000, estimate). After a go decision: lease, build-out, play village and cafe equipment.
