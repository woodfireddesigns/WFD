## 4. SOPs (owner: Jordan; each posted in the back room)

**SOP 1: Opening**
1. Alarm, lights, HVAC. Walk every zone for damage, wet floors, loose parts.
2. Espresso on, dial in, batch brew, milk stocked.
3. Clean bin to floor; sanitize tables, rails, gates, handles, changing tables; stock restrooms.
4. Fill in, sign and post today's cleaning log at the entrance.
5. POS and waiver tablet on; count float; check parties and Group Mornings.
6. Unlock on time.

**SOP 2: Core floor service and the clean standard**
1. Greet within 10 seconds. Shoes off to cubbies.
2. Waiver on file for every child, adult named. Headcount on the board. **25 kids max**; then waitlist by text.
3. Sick-child policy at the door: no fever, vomiting, diarrhea or unexplained rash in 24 hrs. Decline politely with a free-visit voucher.
4. Parents stay on site, always. A child is never left with staff.
5. Mouthed toy goes in the played bin at once; rounds every 20 minutes.
6. Played bin washed and sanitized at nap lull and close; clean bin goes out.
7. Baby Nook is under-2s only.
8. House coffee with every drop-in or pack visit.
9. Every injury or incident in the log: time, name, action.

**SOP 3: A complaint**
1. Listen, thank them, step off the floor if heated.
2. Fix it now: remake, clean, refund the visit.
3. Injury: first aid, incident report, photos, 911 if in doubt; tell Jordan and insurer same day; no written admission of fault.
4. Log it. Jordan answers online reviews within 24 hours, factual, no names.

**SOP 4: Closing**
1. Last call 15 min before close; headcount to zero.
2. Wash, sanitize, air-dry all played-bin toys.
3. Vacuum and mop; sanitize mats, rails, gates, cubbies, high chairs.
4. Backflush espresso, clean grinder, milk away, run dishwasher.
5. Cash up against POS; note low stock.
6. Sign the log, photo to team chat, alarm.

**SOP 5: Weekly reorder (Monday)**
1. Count coffee, milk, oat milk, cups, lids, sanitizer, wipes, snacks, party supplies.
2. Order to par (coffee 1.5 weeks, others 2 weeks): roaster Monday, Sysco by cutoff.
3. Log costs in QuickBooks; flag any price change over 10% to the CFO.

**SOP 6: Party day**
1. 48 hrs before: confirm headcount, allergies; send waiver link to guest adults.
2. **Every child guest stays with an adult; no drop-off parties.**
3. 30 min before: set and sanitize room, platter ready.
4. Host checks waivers, runs the 2 hours.
5. Guests in the village count toward the 25 cap.
6. Reset in 30 min. All-in bill: no gratuity, no card fee.

## 6. Licenses, permits, insurance (confirm every item)

**Key permit risk:** the change of use to an assembly occupancy. Salisbury requires a new Certificate of Occupancy for any change of use or tenant, and the fire marshal and code review can then require sprinklers, alarms, egress and restroom upgrades, plus health-department sinks for the coffee bar. In a former retail box this can blow the build-out budget. Get a pre-lease code review and make the lease contingent on it.

**Why "parents stay, no drop-off" should avoid child care licensing.** MSDE's center rules (COMAR 13A.16) govern **nonparental** care of children in a group setting ([COMAR 13A.16.01.01](https://regulations.justia.com/states/maryland/title-13a/subtitle-16/chapter-13a-16-01/section-13a-16-01-01)). With a parent on site and responsible, care stays parental. I found **no explicit Maryland parent-on-premises exemption** (Delaware has one). **Get a written determination from the MSDE Office of Child Care** before the lease ([MSDE](https://earlychildhood.marylandpublicschools.org/node/265)). Edges to avoid: drop-off parties, "parents' night out," staff taking a child to the bathroom, one adult minding a group's kids.

## 7. Lease checklist

- [ ] Use clause covering play cafe, coffee and private events; **exclusivity** for play cafes and kids' indoor play in the center.
- [ ] **Renewal options:** two 5-year options at set increases.
- [ ] **Contingent on financing and permits** (CO, health plan approval, MSDE letter).
- [ ] **Rent abatement** through build-out; rent starts at CO.
- [ ] **TI allowance** in dollars per sq ft, paid on schedule.
- [ ] **NNN/CAM in writing**, capped, with 2 years of actuals.
- [ ] Prefer a former restaurant or assembly space.
- [ ] HVAC age and replacement; signage rights; stroller parking.
