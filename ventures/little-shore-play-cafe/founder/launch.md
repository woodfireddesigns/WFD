### Turning pop-up numbers into visits a day

**Method A (member ratio).** In the CFO mix, members are about 15% of visits and visit 6 times a month.
Visits a day = deposits x 70% kept to paid membership (estimate) x 6 / 0.15 / 26 = **deposits x 1.08**.
40 deposits = 43 a day. 42 = 45. 50 = 54.

**Method B (market funnel).** Visits a day = [3,500 families within 20 min (CFO estimate) x 80% aware at steady state (estimate) x trial rate x paid conversion x paid visits a month + member visits] / 26.
- Trial rate = unique RSVP families / unique local parents reached (Meta reach + group sizes).
- Paid conversion and visits a month come from pop-ups 3 and 4; cap visits a month at 1.2 (panel: "once or twice a month") unless repeat tickets prove more.
- Three weeks of promotion understate a year of trial, so B is a floor: it must clear break-even (30); A must clear 45.

### Real buyers vs the panel

The panel said 24% would buy (as pitched) and 45% (current offer). The real comparable is **families who paid anything (deposit or ticket) / families who attended.**
- 45%+: the panel held.
- 24% to 44%: partial; go only if every go floor is met.
- Under 24%: below even the original pitch. **Trust the real buyers**, refund, and go back to `/founder-offer` and `/founder-pricing`.
