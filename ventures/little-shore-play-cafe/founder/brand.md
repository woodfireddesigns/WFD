# Brand: Little Shore Play Cafe

Positioning used (marketing.md not yet written): *the calm, clean play cafe where your under-5 plays and you actually sit down, unlike mixed-age bounce venues or a 30-minute storytime.* Re-check against marketing.md when it lands.

**Recommendation: keep Little Shore.** It passed every public check I could run and says place and scale in two words. Its weakness is legal strength, not fit.

## 1. Name candidates

| # | Name | Style | What it says | Out loud / on a sign | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Little Shore Play Cafe** | Place + scale | Small, local, ours; "the Shore" is how locals say Eastern Shore | Soft, two beats, easy to spell; "Little Shore" holds alone on a sign | "Shore" is everywhere locally (Shore Stop, Shore United Bank) and mildly geographic, so a weaker mark; can read "beach" to OC visitors |
| 2 | **Loblolly Play Cafe** | Place (Delmarva's native pine) | Rooted, woodsy, playful sound | Fun to say; kids will repeat it; some will misspell it | Means nothing to newcomers; "loblolly" also means mud puddle |
| 3 | **Heron House Play Cafe** | Evocative | Calm, watchful (a parent who stays), marsh not beach | Warm, alliterative, strong icon (heron) | Herons are common in coastal branding; "House" can hint at care setting |
| 4 | Latte Little Ones | Pun | Coffee for you, play for them | Cute once, tiring on a sign | "Little Lattes Play Cafe" already exists (Coventry, RI): same category, confusing |
| 5 | Spartina | Place (salt-marsh grass) | Calm, natural, upscale | Elegant; nobody knows it | Too spa, not enough kid |
| 6 | Sandbar | Place | Shallow, safe water; a place to stop | Short, good icon | Reads bar/beach; wrong for faith-valued families |
| 7 | [Family name] & Co. Play Cafe | Founder | Family-run, accountable | Personal, trusted locally | Hard to sell later; ties brand to one family |
| 8 | Wicomico Play House | Place | County pride | Long, hard for transplants to say | "Play House" reads childcare; county name feels civic |

**Shortlist: Little Shore Play Cafe, Loblolly Play Cafe, Heron House Play Cafe.**

Also considered: The Play Village (generic, unprotectable), Shorelet (invented, needs explaining).

**Latte Little Ones:** menu only, as the parent coffee + kid steamed milk combo. The Rhode Island "Little Lattes" play cafe makes it a poor public name.

## 3. Voice and promise

**Promise:** A calm, clean place where your little one plays and you actually sit down.

**Taglines:**
1. **They play. You sit. Right there.** *(pick: the parent's half of the promise, and "right there" says parents stay)*
2. Calm play on the Shore.
3. Small hands, slow mornings.

**Voice:**

| Adjective | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Neighborly | "See you Tuesday. Bring the twins." | "Join our community of curated families." |
| Calm | Short sentences, prices up front, no exclamation pile-ups | "OMG the CUTEST new spot!!!" |
| Plainspoken | "Toys get washed after every use. The log is by the door." | "We prioritize hygiene excellence." |

**Before (pitch.md):** "Kids play in a calm play village (pretend market, farm, mini gym, reading nook) plus a gated Baby Nook for under-2s, while you stay a few feet away with coffee and Wi-Fi."

**After:** "Your toddler shops the pretend market. The baby crawls in the Baby Nook. You sit ten feet away with a hot coffee you didn't have to reheat."

**Faith-valued, welcoming to all:** show it in hospitality, not iconography: church-group Group Mornings, names remembered, gracious complaint replies. Keep symbols out of the public mark so no family reads the door as "not for us." Founders' call.

**Answering "a bit Instagram for Salisbury":** real local families (signed releases), daylight, the mess before the cleanup, Jordan's face, prices in posts. No ring light, styled toy flat lays or influencer deals.

## 4. The look

### Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | Spartina | `#3E5A4C` | Marsh-grass green. Wordmark, signage, text on cream |
| Core | Sandbar | `#F4EEE2` | Warm cream. Backgrounds, cups, walls |
| Core | Oak | `#A9744A` | The room's wood. Accent, icon fill, large display only |
| Support | Oak Deep | `#8A5A33` | Oak when it must carry small text |
| Support | Marsh Tint | `#C9D3BF` | Pale sage for zone panels, menu blocks |
| Support | Ink | `#24302A` | Body text when green is too soft |

Contrast (WCAG 2.x, computed):

| Text on background | Ratio | AA normal (4.5) | AA large (3.0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spartina on Sandbar | 6.56 | Pass | Pass |
| Sandbar on Spartina | 6.56 | Pass | Pass |
| White on Spartina | 7.58 | Pass (AAA) | Pass |
| Ink on Sandbar | 11.87 | Pass (AAA) | Pass |
| Oak on Sandbar | 3.44 | **Fail** | Pass |
| Sandbar on Oak | 3.44 | **Fail** | Pass |
| Oak Deep on Sandbar | 5.06 | Pass | Pass |

Rule: Oak never carries text under 24px (18.66px bold). Ownable over the Perdue room's oak/cream/sage: the green is pushed darker and bluer toward salt marsh, so it reads Delmarva, not Scandinavian nursery. No seafoam, navy stripe or coral.

### Type

| Use | Free (Google Fonts, OFL) | Adobe Fonts option (if licensed) |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Fraunces** (Soft axis high, Wonk off, semibold): warm serif, friendly without being cute | Bely Display |
| Text | **Figtree**: open, legible on menus and phones | Sofia Pro |

Default to the Google pair so the brand never depends on a subscription. Outline the final wordmark; confirm Adobe Fonts terms for logo, sign and app embedding before using an Adobe face.

### Logo brief

- **Says:** small, local, calm, coastal-in-spirit. A place on the Shore, not a beach.
- **Mark:** wordmark "Little Shore" in the display face, plus a simple symbol: one low horizon line with three marsh-grass blades (or a wading heron reduced to two strokes). Explore both; choose the one that survives 32px.
- **Lockups:** primary (symbol + wordmark + "Play Cafe" small caps), horizontal (fascia), symbol only, stamp ("Little Shore · Salisbury, MD").
- **Must work on:** fascia at 60+ ft (one color); sleeve (one-color Spartina on kraft); 32px icon (symbol on Spartina square, 3 strokes max, test at 16px); sponsor wall (mark small, at top).
- **Avoid:** anchors, lighthouses, crabs, shells, waves-as-swoosh, Old Bay cues, rainbow primaries, cartoon mascots, script fonts, anything that looks like a daycare sign.
- I can generate concept sketches with an image tool on request; they are concepts, not a logo.

### Sponsors without logos on the play structures

- **Zones are named by place, not by sponsor:** The Market, The Farm Stand, The Marsh (gym), The Reading Nook, The Baby Nook.
- **Each zone gets one small oak plaque**, routed or engraved in Figtree, one size for all: *"The Farm Stand · kept by [Sponsor name]"*. Name only. No logo, no colors, no URL.
- **One sponsor wall** near the cafe counter: oak slats, every name engraved in the same face and color. Tier shows by position (top row = flagship), never by size or logo. Header: *"Neighbors who helped build Little Shore."*
- Logos live off the floor: website sponsor page, an annual thank-you post.
- No name goes up until the sponsor approves in writing. Rise Up and Perdue are targets, not partners.

## 5. First five touchpoints

**1. Sign and homepage.** Fascia: horizontal lockup, no tagline. Hero: real photo of a seated parent with a cup, child mid-play. "They play. You sit. Right there." / "Under 5. Shoes off. Your first visit is free." Button: "Plan your free visit."

**2. Packaging.** Sandbar cup, Spartina symbol, one-color kraft sleeve: "Still hot. You're welcome." Kids' cup (Latte Little Ones combo): symbol only, space for a name in marker.

**3. Confirmation (text + email).**
> You're booked: [day], [time]. Your first visit is free and your coffee's on us.
> Bring socks for everyone (shoes stay at the door). Parents stay on site the whole visit.
> Parking: [details]. Questions: reply here. See you soon. Jordan and the Little Shore family

**4. First social post.** Photo: the empty play village in morning light, Jordan setting out the market baskets. Caption:
> We're building a play cafe in Salisbury for kids under 5 and the grown-ups who bring them. Calm, clean, shoes off, coffee hot. Opening [month, year]. Founding families get $39 a month, locked for a year (first 50). Pop-up dates in our bio.

**5. Reply to the first complaint** (public review, cleanliness):
> [Name], thank you for telling us, and I'm sorry. A sticky toy is exactly what we promise you won't find. I checked our log for [day]: [what the log showed]. We've [specific fix, e.g. added a mid-morning toy swap]. I'd like you to see the difference, so your next visit is on us. Message me directly at [email/phone]. Jordan, owner

Within 24 hours, name the fix, sign with a real name, never argue facts in public.
