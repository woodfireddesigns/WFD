## 3. The stack

**Name: The Little Shore Founding Family Membership**

- **Core:** a calm, clean place your under-5 actually wants to go, and where you actually sit down. Unlimited visits, coffee on us.
- **Bonus 1: Your first visit is free.** Kills "I won't pay for a place I've never seen."
- **Bonus 2: The Baby Nook and Baby & Me mornings.** Kills "big kids running wild around my baby."
- **Bonus 3: Group Mornings at $8 a child.** Turns the moms-group organizer into the sales channel.
- **The guarantee:** cancel in the first 30 days and pay only the drop-in price for the visits you used. Modeled at about $50 a month in refunds.
- **Urgency (true):** $39 a month locked for 12 months, first 50 families or opening day, whichever comes first.
- **The clean promise (proof, not a claim):** posted daily cleaning log, toy rotation, shoes off, sick-child policy, 25 kids max.

## 5. The re-test

`pitch-v1.md` (as pitched) vs `pitch.md` (this offer), same customer profile, seed 2026.

| | as pitched (100 buyers) | this offer (20 buyers) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Buy rate | **24%** | **45%** (40% if the one buyer who only took the free visit is excluded) |
| Baseline reweighted to the re-test's behaviour mix | 24% | |
| Buyers taking the membership in month 1 | ~1 | **0** |
| Non-buyers citing cleanliness | 13 of 76 | 1 of 11 |
| Non-buyers citing "not open / never seen it" | 22 of 76 | 0 of 11 |
| Free alternatives as the objection | 59 of 76 | 11 of 11 |

**What moved:** trust and cleanliness objections collapsed; stay-at-home parents, moms-group organizers and the cleanliness-first segment started buying.

**What did not move:** nobody takes the membership before visiting, and free-activity loyalists and deal hunters still pass. The remaining objection is price per visit against free options; $14 sits right at many buyers' "getting expensive" line. The membership is a retention product for families who already come weekly, not an acquisition product. The CFO model is built that way: 25 member families at steady state, the rest drop-ins and packs.

Caveat: 20 buyers is thin and the cards differ from the first panel. Treat the direction as real and the size as unproven.
