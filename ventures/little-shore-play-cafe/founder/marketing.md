# Marketing: Little Shore Play Cafe

## The job marketing has to do

Nobody in the re-test took the membership before visiting. The membership keeps families; it does not win them. Marketing sells a **free visit** and the ladder does the rest:

Free first visit → $14 drop-in (coffee included) → $55 five-pack → $45 membership ($39 founding, first 50 families).

Two phases:
- **(a) Pop-up campaign (spring 2027, before a lease):** RSVPs to free sessions, then refundable founding deposits. The board wants 40 before a lease.
- **(b) Opening (about Oct 2027):** convert deposits, fill free first visits, pre-sell parties.

## Who we target first (and who we don't)

| segment | panel buy rate (100 / re-test) | role |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Stay-at-home parent | 55% / 75% | Core. Weekday mornings, the hours the model needs filled. |
| Remote or hybrid worker | 30% / 50% | Weekday mornings, Wi-Fi + work bar. |
| Moms-group organizer | 20% / 100% (thin) | The channel, not just the buyer. Group Mornings at $8 a child. |
| Birthday party planner | 60% (thin) / 100% (thin) | Second engine. Pre-sell parties at opening. |

**Not targeting first:** deal hunters and free-activity loyalists (0% in both panels). No Groupon, no free-day bait, no library or park flyer drops. Weekend-only parents (25%) get the 5-pack message, not their own campaign.

## Positioning

Three versions:

1. **For Salisbury parents home with an under-5 all week, who are out of indoor places to go, Little Shore is the calm, clean play cafe where your little one plays a few feet away and you actually sit down with a coffee, unlike the mixed-age bounce floors in town.**
2. For parents of babies and toddlers who worry about big kids and germs, Little Shore is the under-5 play space with a gated Baby Nook and a posted cleaning log, unlike all-ages fun centers.
3. For moms groups who need a weekday spot, Little Shore hosts your group for $8 a child, unlike a living room.

**Recommend 1.** Stay-at-home parents are the top buyer in both panels; "indoor options here are thin" is the lead buy reason; CoCo's top review complaint is big kids, crowding and no parent seating. Version 2 becomes the proof points; version 3 the organizer pitch.

**Short line for signage and bios:** *The calm, clean play cafe where you actually sit down.*

## Rules for the pop-up campaign

- Pop-ups are **free with RSVP**, 25 kids per session. Parents stay on site; no copy implies drop-off.
- Ads sell the **free RSVP**, not the deposit. Deposits are pitched in person and to attendees after.
- Founding offer: $39 a month, locked 12 months, first 50 families or opening day, whichever comes first. [Deposit amount] / [refund terms]: have an attorney confirm the wording before posting (Maryland AG Consumer Protection: https://www.marylandattorneygeneral.gov/Pages/CPD/default.aspx). Not legal advice.
- Pricing's $39 vs $45 test: never show two founding prices in the same groups. Public copy stays $39; test $45, if at all, at a later pop-up elsewhere.
- Every attendee gets a **"first visit free at opening"** card. That is the opening list.

## Ten hooks

Each answers one panel objection. Copy below is ours, not panel quotes.

| # | objection | format | hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Free alternatives (library, zoo, parks) | Reel, first 3 seconds | "Storytime is 30 minutes on Thursday. This is for the rest of the week." |
| 2 | "I'd barely use a membership" | Ad headline | "Don't buy a membership. Come free first, then pay per visit." |
| 3 | Big kids around my baby | Reel / sign | "Under 2? You get your own gated corner." |
| 4 | Is it clean? | Sign by the door / carousel | "Our cleaning log is on the wall. Read it before you take your shoes off." |
| 5 | Never seen it | FB group post headline | "Don't take our word for it. Come see it Saturday, free." |
| 6 | CoCo's with nicer decor | Ad headline | "No bounce houses. No 10-year-olds. On purpose." |
| 7 | Drive and gas | Ad / map graphic | "[X] minutes off Route 13. Closer than you think." (fill after the lease) |
| 8 | Chasing my kid, not drinking coffee | Reel, first 3 seconds | "Every seat sees every zone. Drink it while it's hot." |
| 9 | Weekends only | Carousel | "Saturdays only? The 5-pack: $11 a visit, good for 6 months." |
| 10 | Too Instagram for Salisbury | IG caption opener | "Shoes off, coffee on, prices on one page. That's the whole thing." |

Avoid in all copy: health or "germ-free" claims, child-development outcome claims, "as seen on", follower counts, reviews before there are any.

## What a customer is worth, and the most to pay

**Max cost per acquired customer (first-month payback):**
1.6 visits in month 1 (panel) × $17.05 contribution per family visit (CFO) = **$27.28**.
On the re-test's 1.4 visits: 1.4 × $17.05 = $23.87. **Cap: $27. Working target: $15.**

**6-month value (estimate).** Assumptions: 1.6 visits in month 1; half of first-month buyers keep coming in months 2 to 6 at 1.2 visits a month (panel: "once or twice a month"); $17.05 per visit holds (it is a blended figure that includes party and program margin, so it flatters a drop-in-only family).
1.6 + (5 × 1.2 × 0.5) = 4.6 visits × $17.05 = **$78.43**.
Spend against $78 only after the first 60 days prove repeat visits.

**Pop-up phase:** $2,000 ÷ 50 deposits = **$40 per deposit target**. A founding family pays $39 × 12 = $468 in dues alone, so $40 is safe.

## Three numbers to watch weekly

| number | target | change something when |

| **Cost per RSVP** (paid spend ÷ paid RSVPs) | $5 or less | Over $8 for 5 days: swap creative, then shift money to organizers. Under 60 RSVPs for Pop-up 1 by Wed Apr 7: add a third creator or a second group-post wave. |

| **Cost per founding deposit** (all spend ÷ deposits) | $40 or less | Over $60: cut paid, go organizer-only. Fewer than 20 deposits by Mon Apr 26: report to the board before any lease talk. |
