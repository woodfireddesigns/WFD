The essence, kept on purpose

Clean, modern, enchanting. Then profitable.

No lever was allowed to cost the experience: calm density (about 35 sq ft of play floor per child, never the fire-code max), two people on the floor at all times, a cleaning log parents can see, coffee in every visit, a gated Baby Nook, and a designed play village. Those details are what moved buyers from no to yes in every round.

What is still not solved

Demand decides the rest

  • Debt and owner pay: with a $150k loan and $3,000 a month for Jordan, break-even is 40 visits a day. Year 1 is negative at 45 a day and positive at 55.
  • Simulated buyers are an upper bound. The pop-ups (Feb to May 2027, weekday $13 / weekend $15 exactly as tested) are the real test.
  • Party pre-sales move after the lease: 8 of 10 birthday planners would not book without an opening date.
  • Free-activity loyalists bought at 0% in every round. Do not market to them.
  • Costs are estimates except the rent quote. Get real quotes before lenders see this.
