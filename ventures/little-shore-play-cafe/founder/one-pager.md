# Little Shore Play Cafe · One page

**They play. You sit. Right there.**

**The problem.** Salisbury parents of under-5s run out of indoor places to go. In town it is mixed-age bounce venues or a 30-minute storytime. No play cafe exists within 30 miles.

**The customer.** Stay-at-home parents, remote and hybrid workers, weekend working parents and birthday planners in Salisbury and Wicomico County.

**The offer.** A calm, clean, beautifully designed play village for ages 8 months to 5 with a gated Baby Nook. Parents stay, free, with a real seat and a real coffee. Never packed: we limit how many kids are in at once.

- First visit free
- Weekdays $13, weekends $15, coffee included (siblings $7, under 1 free)
- 5-visit pack $60
- Unlimited $45/month; first 50 founding families $39 locked for a year
- Birthday parties $325 (12 kids) or $395 (20 kids), all-in
- Teacher-led music, movement and art classes $12
