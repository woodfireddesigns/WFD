## What would flip a no

- A free visit that shows the Baby Nook really keeps the bigger kids out and the room stays calm, plus a cheaper weekday-morning drop-in around $8 to $10 that fits around our shifts.
- A drop-in of around $8 per child with a sibling discount that really cuts the cost, or a rainy-day pack that works out under $6 a visit.
- If the Group Mornings at $8 a kid really worked for my moms group and a few other families committed to coming, I'd organize one and pay for that.
- Opening close to me with real reviews from other parents, plus a drop-in price around $8-10 a child, or a pack bringing each visit under $9.
- A much lower drop-in, like $6-7 a kid, or a free-entry day each month. I'd also need to hear a few other moms say it was worth it.
- A quiet, separate under-2 time slot on Saturdays or a way to book a spot with a small capped group, so I know the baby won't get run over.
- A Saturday or rainy-day price closer to $8 a kid, or a family cap per visit, and the free first visit turning out great.
- A great free first visit on a Saturday where my kid actually wants to go back, plus a drop-in or pack price that works for weekend-only use.
- A flat drop-in around $5 to $6 for the first kid, or a free-with-purchase deal on shift days when the parks are too hot or rainy.
- A cheaper pack or a Groupon-style deal, say 5 visits for about $40 that doesn't expire quickly, after a free visit my kids actually love.
- A party package clearly cheaper or easier than CoCo's, or a drop-in price under about $10 per child with the free first visit proving it's actually calmer and cleaner.
- A much lower drop-in price (around $8 for the first child) or a free or cheap open-play hour, so it's not a $20+ outing for two kids.

Buyers say they would buy **1.4 times** in the first month on average.
