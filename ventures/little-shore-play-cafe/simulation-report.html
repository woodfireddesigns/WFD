The headline finding

This is a visit business. The membership is not how families arrive.

Out of 100 simulated Salisbury parents, about 1 leaned toward the $59 membership. On the revised offer, 0 of 20 took a membership before visiting. Every buyer started with a drop-in. The membership becomes a retention tool for families who already come weekly, so the model now runs on 25 member families at steady state and lets drop-ins and 5-packs carry the volume.

Go / no-go number for the pop-ups 45 paying family visits a day

At the 38-a-day plan, the business covers its costs but cannot carry a $150k loan and pay Jordan $3,000 a month. At 45, it clears about $4,000 a month after both. The launch plan turns pop-up deposits into this forecast before anyone signs a lease.
