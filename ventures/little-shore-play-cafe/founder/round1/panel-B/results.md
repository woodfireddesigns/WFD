## What would flip a no

- A weekday drop-in around $8 per child, or a cheap multi-visit pack that works out to under $8 a visit.
- A group rate low enough that my moms group could come for about $5 a kid, or proof from other local parents that it's clearly better than CoCo's.
- A drop-in around $7-8 a kid, or a cheap weekday-morning rate, so a rainy-day visit doesn't feel like a splurge.
- A weekend price around $10 to $12, or a clear way to see that the Baby Nook is kept separate from the 5-year-olds on Saturdays.
- A weekend price closer to $10 per kid, or a bad-weather or winter stretch where the zoo isn't an option and I need somewhere indoors.
- A rainy-day or heat-wave backup with a much lower price, around $6 to $8 a kid, or a free-entry deal tied to a church or hospital group.
- A Groupon or intro deal bringing a visit to about $8 to $9, or a pack that doesn't expire and works on any day including weekday mornings after a night shift.
- A drop-in around $8 a kid, or a real reason it beats free options on bad-weather days, like a punch pass I could use only when it rains.
- A drop-in around $8 for the 4-year-old with the baby free, or a punch pack that works out near $8 a visit, if the place is close enough that gas isn't a big add-on.

Buyers say they would buy **1.6 times** in the first month on average.
