# Wood Fired Designs — Retainer Packages

Reference doc. Drafted September 2026. Everything here is a working position, not a
published price list.

---

## Who this is for

Wood Fired Designs is Michael Deschenes, one person, operating since 2014. Sixty-plus
brand systems shipped across food and CPG, apparel, hospitality and sports. Creative
credits include the Masters Tournament, the US Open and American Needle.

The work spans brand identity, packaging with print-ready dielines, AI product
photography, Shopify and Framer builds, and paid and organic ad creative. One person
does all of it, which is why the bottle, the product page and the ad end up speaking
the same way without a document having to enforce it.

Delivery speed is unusual. A full brand system, packaging, storefront and ad set that
an agency scopes at four months runs closer to thirty working hours here. That speed
is margin, not a discount.

## The positioning problem these packages solve

A "website maintenance plan" gets compared to a $99 WordPress care plan, because that
is the nearest thing a buyer can price it against. That comparison caps the category
near $1,200 a month no matter how good the work is.

The fix is to sell an outsourced creative department instead: a countable stack of
output the client can price against the agency quote already sitting in their drawer.
Same work, different shelf, roughly triple the number.

## The three tiers

Always present all three. Most clients take the middle one, which is why the middle
one carries the number worth having.

### Foundation — $1,500/month

- 6 static ad creatives per month
- Site updates and health checks
- Product and content refreshes
- Monthly note on what shipped

About 5 hours. Effective rate $300/hr.

### Studio — $3,500/month

**The tier most clients land on.**

- 15 ad creatives per month, including 2 video
- 2 email campaigns, designed and built
- 1 landing page or PDP per quarter
- AI product photography as needed

About 12 hours. Effective rate $292/hr.

### Partner — $6,500/month

- 25+ creatives, video-led
- 4 email campaigns

About 20 hours. Effective rate $325/hr.

## Specialist track: brand stewardship

Some engagements are not creative production. They are holding a system together while
other people build against it.

On a multi-SKU launch with a manufacturing chain, the work is authority order, lockup
and palette enforcement, naming across registers, a render control sheet, SKU
architecture, and a drift register catching what would otherwise go to print wrong.

**$1,500/month** as a standalone track, or folded into Studio or Partner.

Sold standalone it is usually underpriced, because the value shows up as errors that
never happened. When a client has three or more suppliers producing against the system,
price it inside Partner instead.

## Rules for the close

**Never quote one number.** A single price invites yes or no. Three prices invite a
choice, and the choice is almost always the middle.

**Sell the retainer inside the project, not after it.** Add a final phase to every
proposal covering what happens after launch, priced. A proposal that ends at go-live
means selling the same client again cold in ninety days, after the relationship has
cooled.

**Three-month minimum, then month to month.** Long enough to show results, short enough
that nobody has to think hard about signing. Annual prepay at ten percent off for anyone
who takes it, which also smooths cash flow.

**Count the deliverables out loud.** "Fifteen creatives, two campaigns, a landing page"
is checkable. "Ongoing creative support" is not, and unpriceable things get priced low.

**Nothing below $1,500.** A $600 client expects the same responsiveness as a $3,500 one
and occupies the same emotional space. Below that line the retainer is a job, not a
business.

**Handover is the sell moment.** The day the site goes live and the final invoice lands
is the highest-leverage conversation of the engagement. After that, every week costs
warmth.

## Pricing the project that precedes the retainer

Retainers follow projects, so the project price sets the anchor.

A full brand system, four print-ready package labels with dielines, a complete Shopify
build and an ad template set is **$14,000 to $16,000**. Not $7,000. If a client posts a
budget range, price inside the upper half of it rather than under the bottom.

Line items worth knowing, because they are routinely underquoted:

| Deliverable | Realistic |
|---|---|
| Investor pitch deck, 15–18 slides | $3,000–5,000 |
| Shopify homepage | $2,000–3,500 |
| Full identity system with guidelines | $4,000–8,000 |
| Print-ready CPG label with dieline | $600–900 each |

Hourly for out-of-scope work: **$75**. Note that a fixed-price engagement delivered in
thirty hours at $14,500 is an effective rate near $480, which is the argument for never
pricing on hours.

## Where retainer clients come from

Ranked by speed to close.

1. **The back catalogue.** Sixty-plus brands already shipped. Warmest list available,
   and none of them have ever been sent a retainer offer.
2. **Live and recent clients.** Upgrade conversations at handover.
3. **Project-to-retainer conversion.** Built into the proposal from day one.
4. **Referrals** from happy retainer clients and from adjacent professional services.
5. **Cold, qualified.** Brands with a good product and mediocre packaging that are
   already spending on paid. The bad packaging is the qualifier, because it is the
   problem that can be visibly solved in a single image.

The opener that works is not a pitch. It is a complete unsolicited rebuild of the
brand — repositioned packaging, a product shot, an ad frame, a storefront section —
sent with no ask attached. A critique triggers defensiveness. A rebuild triggers
desire, and it demonstrates the entire offer in one image.
