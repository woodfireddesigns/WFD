Little Shore Play Cafe, a new Montessori-inspired play cafe opening in Salisbury, MD next year.
Kids 8 months to 5 play in a calm, designed play village (pretend market, farm, mini gym, reading nook) while you stay a few feet away with coffee, light snacks and Wi-Fi. Parents stay on site; no drop-off.
Unlimited monthly membership (the main way to join): $59 a month for your first child, +$15 for each additional child. Founding families (first 40 only): $49 a month, locked for 12 months.
Drop-in: $12 first child, $8 each sibling, under 1 free, plus one drink per adult.
Also: story time, music hour, kid yoga, moms nights, and 2-hour birthday parties from $275.
