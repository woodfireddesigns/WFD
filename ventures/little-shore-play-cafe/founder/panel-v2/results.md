## What would flip a no

- Dedicated quiet hours for under-2s or a separate baby-only session, and a drop-in closer to $8-10 so it fits when I need a work-from-home morning.
- If it were close to home or my daycare route, or if the 5-visit pack came in under about $40. Seeing reviews from other parents saying it really stays clean would also help.
- A drop-in price around $7 to $8 for me and the kids, or a free community day each month. Then I might try it once.
- Quiet, age-separated Saturday sessions for under-2s, or a lower drop-in price around $8 to $9 on weekends.
- A weekend price closer to $8-10 a visit, or a drop-in that works out cheaper than a trip to the zoo on a rainy or hot Saturday.
- Weekend hours that are reliably open, and a drop-in closer to $10 or a pack price that makes a Saturday visit feel cheap.
- A very cheap or free option, like free weekday hours or a church-group rate, or a rainy-day need the library can't cover.
- A Groupon or similar deal, like a few visits for under $30, plus proof after the free visit that my 4-year-old and the baby both stay happy there.
- A Groupon or a 5-visit pack at around $35-40, or an off-peak shift-worker weekday rate that makes each visit about $8-9.
- A real rainy-day or heat-wave use for it, like a cheap drop-in on bad weather days, or a church or moms-group outing where the Group Morning $8 per kid is split and I'm just tagging along. The free first visit might get me in once, but I'd probably stop there.
- A cheaper per-visit price for two kids (around $10 total), or a location close to me, so a visit costs less than the gas and the fee together.

Buyers say they would buy **1.4 times** in the first month on average.
