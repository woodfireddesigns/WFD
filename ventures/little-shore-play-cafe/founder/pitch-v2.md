Little Shore Play Cafe, a new Montessori-inspired play cafe opening in Salisbury, MD next year, built for kids 8 months to 5.
Kids play in a calm play village (pretend market, farm, mini gym, reading nook) plus a gated Baby Nook for under-2s, while you stay a few feet away with coffee and Wi-Fi. Parents stay on site; no drop-off. Capped at 25 kids at a time, shoes off, toys rotated out and cleaned after use, with a posted daily cleaning log.
Your first visit is free. After that: drop-in $14 with house coffee or tea included, siblings $7, under 1 free. Or a 5-visit pack for $55.
Membership: $45 a month, unlimited, month to month, cancel anytime. Founding families (first 50 only) pay $39, locked for 12 months. Not sure? Cancel in the first 30 days and pay only the drop-in price for the visits you used.
Also: weekday Group Mornings for moms groups ($8 a child when 4+ families come together), story time, music hour, and all-in birthday parties from $295 with no surprise fees.
