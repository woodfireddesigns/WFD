## What would flip a no

- A drop-in price around $8-10, or a baby-only time slot so I know the older kids aren't there.
- A visit price around $8 per kid, or a monthly plan under $30 that covers siblings, after I see the first free visit is actually clean and not crowded.
- A drop-in price around $8-10 per kid, or the moms group $8 rate working on evenings and weekends around my shifts.
- A price of about $10 a visit, or a drop-in that costs less on a second kid. Or reviews from real parents saying it really is as clean as claimed.
- A weekend price around $8 to $10 per kid, or a cheap weekend-friendly pass, plus proof it is actually clean and uncrowded on Saturdays.
- A drop-in price around $6 to $8 per child, or something that lets my kid play on their own while I actually sit.
- A Groupon or a 5-visit pack at about $30, or a founding rate under $25 a month for both kids, after a free trial day that actually impressed me.
- A Groupon or a first-month deal, like 5 visits for $40 or less that doesn't expire quickly.
- A drop-in around $8 to $10 per child, or proof that it's really cleaner and calmer than the other indoor places I've tried.
- A $39 founding rate or a drop-in closer to $8-10, and it being close enough that the drive isn't a big gas cost.

Buyers say they would buy **1.7 times** in the first month on average.
