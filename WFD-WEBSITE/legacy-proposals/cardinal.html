/ The Cardinal Challenge

You're pitching hospitals, property managers, and institutions. They decide in seconds.

01

Your client doesn't want eye-catching — they want confidence

Commercial property managers and facilities directors aren't scanning Facebook ads. They're vetting vendors. A brand that looks buttoned-up and institutional instills trust before a single conversation happens.

02

You're brand new — your brand can't look like it

Cardinal is launching in July. Your competition has years of signage, trucks, and reputation on the street. The right visual identity closes that gap immediately — and makes you look like you've been doing this for a decade.

03

You're building to sell — brand equity is part of the exit

Private equity doesn't just buy revenue. They buy systems, processes, and brand value. A clean, professional, ownable brand identity signals that Cardinal is built to scale — not just a couple guys with a truck.

/ How It Works for Cardinal

A tight, direct process built around how you actually work. You send me the AI-generated branding brief from your partner sessions — I take it from there. No wasted meetings. No mystery.

01

Brief

You send the branding memo your team has already generated. I absorb it, sharpen the ax, and come back with focused questions — not a long questionnaire starting from scratch.

02

Concepts

I present 3 curated, diverse logo directions — each with a clear rationale. Not a wall of rough sketches. Not 20 options that paralyze a decision. Three that are actually worth choosing between.

03

Refine

You and your partners align internally and relay feedback through one point of contact. I refine until it's right — real revisions, not endless back-and-forth.

04

Deliver

Every file, every format. Brand guidelines, source files, exports ready for trucks, uniforms, and whatever the fleet grows into. Cardinal owns all of it.
